# Texture credits

All maps are NASA imagery, which is not copyrighted (public domain in the United States).
Converted to WebP (2048 × 1024; clouds 1024 × 512) for the web.

| File | Source |
|---|---|
| `earth_day.webp` | NASA Earth Observatory, *Blue Marble: Next Generation*, December 2004, topography and bathymetry (image record 73909) |
| `earth_night.webp` | NASA Earth Observatory, *Black Marble 2016*, city lights at 0.1° (image record 144898) |
| `earth_clouds.webp` | NASA Visible Earth, *Blue Marble* cloud composite, 2048 px (image record 57747) |

The Sun, Venus and Mercury have no textures: they are drawn by shaders in `src/scene/materials.ts`.
